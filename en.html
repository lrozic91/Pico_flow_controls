Case study

Pico Flow Controls

A Zagreb engineering and manufacturing company for the gas and oil industry. It was formed by merging three companies: Feromihin (founded 1992), Macel plin (2002) and PFC (2014). In 2025, its first year as one company, it had €39.4m in revenue.22116

Timeline

From three separate companies to one

MilestoneMerger
  1. 1992.

    Feromihin founded

    On the Martinac family farm in Obedišće, 19 February 1992. It later moved to Novoselec. It started with installing and maintaining pipeline block valve stations and machining spare parts for high-pressure pumps; its largest customers were INA and Plinacro.6075053

  2. 2002.

    Macel plin founded

    20 December 2002 in Zagreb, for maintaining and installing gas systems. From 2010 it is owned by Miroslav Dombrovski and Pavle Grđan.1011

  3. 2014.

    PICO FLOW CONTROLS founded

    17 October 2014. Founders: Pavle Grđan, Miroslav Dombrovski and Dubravko Kamenečki.3

  4. 2015.

    PFC takes over Macel plin

    From 2 January 2015 PFC is the sole owner of Macel plin. Kamenečki becomes PFC's director on 30 January 2015.113

  5. 2016.

    PFC buys Feromihin

    Owner Stjepan Martinac exits the business, and PFC owns the company from 1 August 2016.850

  6. 2017.

    Fahlke and new certifications

    Feromihin is the authorised distributor and service partner in Croatia for Germany's Fahlke Control Systems (block valve station equipment). Alongside ISO 9001 and ISO 29001 it gains ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001 and SCC.60

  7. 2020.

    MRS Magadenovac for Plinacro

    Final commissioning of a measuring and reducing station; investor Plinacro.43

  8. 2021.

    AYMO, first self-service CNG station, MEGC

    The pipeline software is renamed AYMO. Macel plin builds Croatia's first self-service CNG station (Slavonski Brod) and delivers the first gas containers to the Brod Oil Refinery.514139

  9. 2022.

    EU project and exports

    A €1.29m project to commercialise Feromihin's innovations, with €0.36m of EU funding. Feromihin has 40 employees and exports to eight countries.2050

  10. 2023.

    Feromihin doubles revenue

    Revenue grows from €6.25m to €13.75m and headcount from 40 to 54.97

  11. 2023.

    Vranci merger

    On 3 November 2023 VRANCI d.o.o., based at Malešnica 3C, the address of shareholder MDM USLUGE 1991, was merged into PFC; registered on 25 November 2023.113

  12. 2024.

    In-house IT and software

    In January 2024 Luka Klancir, until then CEO of Async Labs, becomes PFC's IT Director. ASYNC d.o.o., owned by PFC, stops operating in April 2024. My conclusion: software development (AYMO) moved into PFC.596

  13. 2024.

    Merger

    The merger agreement for Macel plin and Feromihin into PFC was signed on 6 December 2024, and the assemblies of all three companies voted unanimously; the merger was registered on 19 December 2024. The merged companies were deleted on 11 March 2025.121710

  14. 2025.

    First year as one company

    Revenue €39.37m, net profit €11.30m and 116 employees.24

  15. 2026.

    KPPP, ptc Berlin, successful appeal

    PFC presents the KPPP mobile compressor at HSUP and HUNIG, and with a Turkish partner wins an appeal against a Plinacro tender decision.464755

Business · four areas

Everything for a pipeline, from devices to software

PFC makes its own devices and equipment, builds custom gas plants, maintains and tests pipelines and develops software to manage them. Its main customers are Croatian energy companies.1664

1. Manufacturing

Own devices and pipeline equipment
  • eLBC: an electronic device that closes the valve when a pipeline breaks, also in a solar version. “1000 eLBC installed”.2250
  • RIKI: a calibration device for LBC units.23
  • Printed circuit boards: two SMT lines, 71,600 components per hour in total; also for outside customers.24
  • Pressure equipment: filters, separators, vessels and heat exchangers (ASME VIII, EN 13445, PED).26
  • AEGIS: plastic mesh for pipe protection, geogrids and fencing, made entirely in Croatia; shown at K 2025.2567
  • Pipe segments and steel structures (EN 13480, EN 1090-2), CNC machining and control cabinets (DCS/ESD).66

2. Gas solutions

Custom plants, from design to commissioning
  • KPPP: a mobile recompression unit that pumps gas out of a pipeline section instead of venting it; inlet up to 250 bar, up to 9,000 Sm³/h, 400 kW engine.29
  • Virtual pipeline: compressed gas containers (MEGC) on semi-trailers, 5,700 Nm³ per container; 14 delivered.30
  • Measuring and reducing stations: 30 stations, up to 250 bar.31
  • CNG, LPG, LNG and H2: six CNG stations and one self-service station.32
  • GRIZO synthetic gas stations, gas dehydration (5,000–100,000 Sm³/h), odorisation and analysers (15 chromatographs).653433

3. Field services

Testing, repairs and pipeline maintenance
  • Valve testing up to 650 bar (API 6A, 6D, 598) and safety valves up to 400 bar as an accredited inspection body.3536
  • Ballooning and hot tapping: work on a pipeline without interrupting supply, DN 80–200.38
  • Corrosion protection: exclusive Stopaq distributor for Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Slovenia, Serbia, Montenegro and Kosovo.37
  • Pipeline inspections (ILI and non-ILI), electrical works, process instrumentation and civil works.64

4. Software

Digital products for pipeline management
  • AYMO: a pipeline integrity management system with seven modules. Developed with Async Labs and shown in Kazakhstan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Brazil and Berlin.275149
  • AMS: an asset management system; 46,013 assets entered and 3,857 tasks, also used by the LNG terminal.28

Questions for further analysis

Answers need the detailed RGFI statements

  • Why is 2025 revenue (€39.4m) almost double the combined 2024 figure for Feromihin, Macel plin and PFC (~€21m)? Is it one large project, a new customer or different accounting after the merger?
  • In 2024 the three companies had about 159 employees combined, while PFC had 116 in 2025. Where is the difference: departures, contractors or a different count?
  • An EBITDA margin of 38.5% is very high for an engineering company. How much of it is one-off?
  • Shareholders KALIDOR TECHNO and MDM USLUGE 1991 made €4.85m and €1.65m in 2025 with no staff. This points to dividends from PFC, but that is my assumption.

First conclusions

My assessment based on the research, not the company's plan

Own products and know-how

Strength

eLBC, KPPP, MEGC, AYMO and AMS are its own products, not resale. That explains the high margins: 21–24% net even before the merger.912

EU methane regulation

Opportunity

Regulation (EU) 2024/1787 limits methane venting. KPPP lets gas be pumped out of a pipeline section instead of vented, and PFC highlights this in its 2026 presentations.47

Exporting software and devices

Opportunity

AYMO has been shown at fairs from Kazakhstan to Brazil, and Feromihin exported to 8 countries. The statements do not show today's export share.5051

Dependence on few customers

Risk

Partners on the website are mostly Croatian state energy companies (Plinacro, INA, HEP, LNG Hrvatska). Individual customers' shares are not public.64
